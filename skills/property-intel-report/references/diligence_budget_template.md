# Diligence Budget — Baseline Template

The skill produces an itemized diligence budget on slide 10. This file is the master line-item list with regional adjustments. Copy from here, then add or remove based on the property's specifics.

## Regional overlays

### Hurricane coast (FL, AL coast, MS, LA, TX coast, NC, SC, GA coast)

Always include:
- 4-point inspection
- Wind mitigation inspection
- Roof Fortified inspection (if roof < 10 years old)

### Wildfire WUI (CA, parts of CO/AZ/NV/OR/WA, mountain west)

Always include:
- Defensible space evaluation (often free from local fire dept)
- Roof material verification (Class A required for some carriers)

### Cold-climate (MN, WI, MI, NY upstate, ME, VT, NH)

Always include:
- Heating system inspection (boiler, furnace, oil tank if applicable)
- Buried oil tank scan (homes pre-1980 with possible oil heat history)

### Earthquake zones (CA, AK, parts of WA/OR/NV/UT)

Consider:
- Foundation bolting / cripple wall inspection
- Earthquake retrofit eligibility report

### Older homes (pre-1978)

Always include:
- Lead-based paint inspection
- Plumbing material verification (galvanized? polybutylene?)
- Knob-and-tube electrical verification

### Pre-1996 homes

Consider:
- Polybutylene plumbing inspection (used 1978-1996; class action settled, often replacement-grade)
- Federal Pacific / Zinsco panel check (recall items)

### Rural / well & septic

Always include:
- Water quality test (bacteria, coliform, nitrates, lead — full panel)
- Well flow / yield test
- Septic tank pump + drainfield evaluation
- County health septic permit pull
